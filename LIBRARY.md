# Deck organization

Pin any deck to keep it in the top Pinned section, even before studying it. Pins and organization preferences are stored with your browser progress and included in progress backups.

Needs practice contains studied decks with recent Again or Hard ratings. Other studied decks appear in Studied. Decks without any rated cards are kept in the expandable Not started collection; pinning lifts them out of that group. Search expands matching unopened decks automatically.

Organize by Hardest to remember, Most due cards, or Deck name. Pins always stay first. Difficulty averages each studied card's last ten ratings (Again = 1, Hard = 0.7, Good/Easy = 0), then averages those scores across the deck. Unseen cards do not affect difficulty. Successful reviews lower the score. With older saved progress, learning cards count as difficult until new ratings are available; review cards start with no difficulty estimate.

Organization does not change scheduled due dates or unlock future reviews.
